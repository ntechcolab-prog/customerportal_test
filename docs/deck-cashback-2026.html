Rule #1 in B2B

The balance belongs to the company, not the buyer

Personal rewards can conflict with procurement and anti-bribery rules. Each role sees only what it needs.

Admin

Sees history and expiry

Buyer

Sees and uses the balance

Approver

Sees "credit applied" on approval

Technician

Doesn't see it
