Executive summary

Industrial competitors don't offer cashback. Grainger, McMaster-Carr, Würth, Zoro and Uline compete on free shipping, payment terms and easy reorder. A cashback program would be a real differentiator for the portal.

In B2B, the balance belongs to the company, not the buyer. Personal rewards can conflict with procurement and anti-bribery rules. Shopify B2B enforces company-level credit.

The best UX comes from consumer products (Apple Card, Nubank, Mercado Livre, Starbucks, Sephora): balance always visible, earnings shown before and after buying, credit applied with one toggle.

B2B considerations

Ownership and roles

  • Company-owned balance, shared by all Buyers.
  • Admin sees the history and expiry.
  • Buyer sees and applies the balance.
  • Approver sees "Credit applied: −€X" when approving.
  • Technician doesn't see the balance.

Process and finance

  • Approval: credit is applied before approval and locked.
  • Budget: charged net of credit.
  • Invoice: credit as a discount line before VAT, also on the PO.
  • Returns: the pending state handles clawback.
  • Expiry: 12 months is common in B2B; check local rules.

Recommendation for the prototype

Earn
Flat 2% on grinding beads and spare parts. No tiers. Services excluded in v1.

The company account.

Rewards vs. payment terms, boosted items, per-site balances.
