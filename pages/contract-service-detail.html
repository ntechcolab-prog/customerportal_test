Preventive Maintenance Scope

During preventive maintenance, the following activities are performed across 45+ inspection points:

  • Thorough inspection of instrumentation and locking interstices
  • Sealing liquid verification and cleaning (if applicable)
  • Mechanical seal condition assessment
  • Vibration analysis (empty and with product)
  • Shaft runout measurement
  • Drive system inspection including belt and pulley tension
  • Wear part inspection and measurement (pins, discs, vessel, rotor, screen)
  • Process pump condition assessment (if applicable)
  • Replacement identification and stock recommendations
